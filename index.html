Privacy Policy

Self Audit Expense Tracker

Package: com.whattheapp.selfauditexpensetracker Effective Date: March 30, 2025

1. Overview & Core Privacy Commitment

Self Audit Expense Tracker is built with a fundamental commitment to user privacy. We believe your financial transaction data is personal and strictly private.

100% On-Device Local Processing: All SMS and RCS message reading, message parsing, transaction categorization, and expense calculations take place entirely locally on your Android device. No financial data ever leaves your device.

  • No Cloud Servers: We do not operate remote servers to collect, upload, or process your messages or financial records.
  • No Data Sharing or Sales: Your data is never shared with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • No Remote Analytics: We do not use third-party tracking or analytics SDKs to monitor your app usage.

2. Information Processed Locally

To automatically track and audit your expenses, the app processes the following information strictly on your local device:

  • SMS & RCS Messages: Messages received from financial institutions (banks, credit card issuers, payment services) containing debit or credit transaction alerts.
  • App Notifications: Notifications generated by banking and digital wallet apps, accessed via Android's Notification Listener Service.
  • Local Expense Records: Parsed data such as transaction amounts, merchant names, dates, and account categories.

All data is saved exclusively inside the app's isolated local SQLite database (Room framework) on your device.

3. Android Permissions & Usage

To enable local transaction detection and expense management features, the app requests the following Android permissions:

Permission Purpose & Local Usage Scope
android.permission.READ_SMS Scans SMS inbox locally to detect past bank transaction alerts and create local expense entries.
android.permission.RECEIVE_SMS Listens for incoming bank SMS alerts in real-time to automatically log new expenses on-device.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Displays local device notifications for instant expense logging alerts and daily audit summaries.
android.permission.BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE
(NotificationListenerService)		 Detects incoming banking app and RCS notifications locally to capture non-SMS payment alerts (e.g., UPI, digital wallets).

Note: None of the data accessed via these permissions is ever transmitted off your device.

4. Zero Third-Party Sharing

We maintain a zero-sharing architecture:

  • We do not share any information with third parties.
  • We do not monetize or sell any user data.
  • We do not integrate third-party advertising or telemetry SDKs.

5. Data Storage, Retention & Security

  • Storage: All expense logs, custom tags, and budget settings are saved in private device storage.
  • Control & Deletion: You have complete control over your data. You can edit or delete any expense record at any time inside the app, or clear all data via Android Settings.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling the application completely deletes all locally stored database records from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Self Audit Expense Tracker is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.
